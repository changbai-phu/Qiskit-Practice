
## Classical information
### Classical operations
- Deterministic operations - using probabilistic vectors to represent classical state
  - matrix-vector multiplication
  - Dirac notation
    - column vector
    - row vector
- Probabilistic operations - using Stochastic Matrices to represent 
  - Stochastic matrices:
    - all entries are real number and greater or equal to 0
    - the sum of the each column = 1
  - Matrix multiplication
    - associative (the parenthesis)
    - NOT commutative

## Quantum information
Quantum states - using vectors to represent
- vectors are:
  - entries are complex number
  - the sum of absolute value squared of the entries = 1
- **Euclidean Norm** 
- superposition is the linear combination of standard basis states 
- bra is conjugate transpose of ket
- squared of absolute value of complex number = squared of real number + squared of complex number amplitude

### Quantum operations
- Unitary operations
  - Unitary: if $U U^\dagger = U^\dagger U = I$
- Pauli operations
- Hadamard operations
- Phase operations
- Matrix multiplication
